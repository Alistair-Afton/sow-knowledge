## Notes

- `_SO_Script` classes are the scenario ScriptObjects; sibling
  `*_QR_*` top-level functions are the quest-row glue
  (Start/Fail/Complete/CleanupQuest), and `*_SR*`/`_SC_` entries
  are spawn-condition / reset scripts.
- `C1DeathRelay`/`C2DeathRelay`, `Dominate`, `MissionEnd`,
  `FirstPresentationEnd`, `DisableSet`/`EnableSet` recur across
  families — those are the handlers the sim's Lua surface should
  dispatch to.
